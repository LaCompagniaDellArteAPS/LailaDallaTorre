07 / About
Building organizations capable of change.

A career spanning global procurement, process leadership, Centers of Excellence and enterprise transformation has increasingly converged around one challenge: turning strategic ambition into organizational capability.

Experience across strategy execution, operating-model design, process governance, digital transformation, analytics, organizational change, risk and sustainability provides a broad perspective on how complex organizations actually move.

Global teams and cross-functional transformation portfolios have brought that work across organizational and geographic boundaries — from defining direction and governance to building the structures required for execution.

The next evolution is already underway: data, AI and agentic technologies are changing not only tools and processes, but how organizations make decisions, distribute accountability and create value.

The opportunity is not simply to adopt new technology. It is to design organizations capable of using it well.
